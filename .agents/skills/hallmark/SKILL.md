# Hallmark: Artisan UI Framework & Slop-Test Gates

Hallmark ensures that web interfaces generated by AI assistants possess genuine craftsmanship ("Artisan UI"), featuring structural diversity and eliminating machine-made fingerprints.

---

## 1. The Four Core Verbs

### Verb 1: `Build` (Bespoke UI Generation)
* Generates a unique layout tailored to the specific domain and content requirements.
* Chooses a distinct **Macrostructural Archetype** rather than defaulting to standard stacked containers:
  * **The Editorial Broadsheet:** Large serif typography, authoritative column rules, high-density data sidebars, and clean article layouts.
  * **The Asymmetric Split:** Bold, sticky hero statement on one flank with a progressive narrative flow on the other.
  * **The Bento Metric Matrix:** Interlocking cards of varying aspect ratios tailored to highlight hero KPIs alongside secondary evidence.
  * **The Minimalist Ribbon:** Pure negative space, razor-sharp hairline dividers, and stark typographic clarity.

### Verb 2: `Study <URL or Screenshot>`
* Analyzes a target reference (URL, image, or design artifact) to extract its underlying **Design DNA**:
  * Primary & secondary color anchors (hex values and contrast ratios).
  * Font pairing relationship (display vs. text vs. technical data).
  * Spacing density, border treatments, and elevation methodology.
  * Synthesizes findings into a portable design brief.

### Verb 3: `Audit <Target File>`
* Evaluates an existing page against the **25 Hallmark Slop Tests**:
  * *Test 1 (Hero Cliché):* Is the hero just a centered headline, subhead, and two buttons?
  * *Test 2 (Card Uniformity):* Are there multiple identical cards placed side-by-side with no visual hierarchy?
  * *Test 3 (Semantic Color):* Are colors functional (signaling growth, alerts, categories) or merely decorative?
  * *Test 4 (Data Prominence):* Are quantifiable proof points (revenue, percentages, scale) given visual weight over generic marketing copy?
* Outputs a prioritized punch list of improvements without modifying code.

### Verb 4: `Redesign <Target File>`
* Rebuilds the visual layer of an existing page:
  * Preserves 100% of the underlying business logic, copy, IDs, links, and data bindings.
  * Strips away generic AI skin and applies a cohesive artisan aesthetic.
  * Verifies responsive fidelity on both mobile viewport (360px–430px) and desktop (1200px+).

## 2. Genre Themes

Hallmark categorizes interfaces into distinct, cohesive visual genres:
* **Editorial / Academic:** Inspired by *The New York Times*, *Financial Times*, and *Harvard Business Review*. Serif headings (`Playfair Display`, `Newsreader`), warm ivory paper tones (`#FAFAF8`, `#FFFDF7`), hairline borders (`#E2DFD9`), and precise mono data stamps.
* **Modern Minimal:** High-contrast monochrome, ultra-clean geometry, generous whitespace, subtle 1px border cards, and crisp sans-serif typography.
* **Atmospheric Dark:** Deep obsidian surfaces (`#0D0D0E`), directional soft ambient glows, subtle border illumination, and vibrant accent highlights.
* **Playful Tactical:** Distinctive pastel accents, tactile pill buttons, subtle pop shadows, and energetic rhythm.

## 3. Pre-Emit Self-Critique Gate

Before emitting any frontend code, the agent MUST run this internal critique:
1. "If a human designer looked at this, could they tell in 3 seconds that it was generated by an AI?"
2. "Is the hierarchy undeniable? Does the viewer's eye know exactly where to look first, second, and third?"
3. "Are all tags, buttons, and metrics snug and intentional, with zero accidental wrapping or awkward line breaks?"
4. "Is every section distinctly memorable while feeling part of one unified brand?"
